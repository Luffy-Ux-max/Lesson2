Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

Dr. Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam was a famous Indian scientist, aerospace engineer, author, and the 11th President of India. He was born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu.

Early Life and Education

Dr. Kalam came from a simple family and worked hard to achieve his dreams. He studied physics at Saint Joseph's College and later aerospace engineering at the Madras Institute of Technology. His life showed that hard work, discipline, and education can help people achieve great success.

Scientific Achievements

Dr. Kalam worked with important organisations such as ISRO and DRDO. He was the project director of the SLV-III launch vehicle, which successfully placed the Rohini satellite into orbit in 1980.

He also played an important role in developing India's Agni and Prithvi missiles. Because of his contribution to India's missile programme, he was popularly known as the "Missile Man of India."

Dr. Kalam contributed to the Pokhran-II nuclear tests conducted in 1998. His scientific achievements helped strengthen India's space and defence programmes.

Awards and Honours

  • He received the Padma Bhushan in 1981.
  • He received the Padma Vibhushan in 1990.
  • He received the Bharat Ratna in 1997.
  • He served as the President of India from 2002 to 2007.
  • He was lovingly called the "People's President."

Books Written by Dr. Kalam

Dr. Kalam wrote many inspiring books, including Wings of Fire, Ignited Minds, and India 2020. These books encouraged students to dream big and work for the development of the nation.

His Message to Students

“Dream big, work hard, and never give up.”

Dr. Kalam was only a scientist a scientist, teacher, author, and inspiring national leader. He encouraged young people to use knowledge and technology to build a better India.

Dr. Kalam passed away on 27 July 2015 while delivering a lecture at IIM Shillong. His achievements and ideas continue to inspire millions of people. Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam is remembered as the Missile Man of India and the People's President.
